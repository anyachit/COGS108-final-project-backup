College Major & Early-Career Employment Outcomes

Overview
This COGS 108 project examines the relationship between undergraduate major and early-career employment outcomes among U.S. college graduates. Using the 2023 National Survey of College Graduates (NSCG), we investigate whether major category is associated with differences in degree-occupation match, salary, labor force status, and employment stability. 

Research Question
To what extent do undergraduate major categories, particularly fields related to Science and Engineering, predict stability in early-career employment among U.S. college graduates since 2016? This study evaluates how varying major fields in Science and Engineering differ in employment versus unemployment rates and occupation major match, assessing whether certain major categories are associated with stronger workforce tenure or greater mismatch vulnerabilities. A secondary goal would be assessing whether certain major categories are associated with stronger workforce tenure or greater mismatch vulnerabilities as an additional indicator of employment stability.

Data
Dataset: 2023 National Survey of College Graduates (NSCG)
Provider: National Center for Science and Engineering Statistics (NCSES)

The final analysis uses approximately 11,206 respondents after filtering and cleaning the original dataset.


Methods & Tools
Data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, visualization, and statistical testing were conducted in Python/Jupyter Notebook using:

- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- SciPy
- Statsmodels

Statistical methods included Chi-Square tests, Cramér's V, Kruskal-Wallis testing, and Tukey HSD post-hoc analysis.

For detailed methodology, results, visualizations, and discussion, see the numbered Jupyter notebooks in this repository.
